# CarbonFlow

CarbonFlow is an enterprise blockchain solution that revolutionizes carbon tracking by creating a comprehensive accountability network across supply chains. Unlike traditional systems that rely on periodic reporting and estimations, CarbonFlow tokenizes carbon emissions at every touchpoint and maintains dynamic carbon passports that update automatically as products move through the supply chain. The platform introduces Carbon Velocity Tokens (CVT) to represent real-time carbon accumulation rates, enabling predictive modeling and automated compliance monitoring.

The system leverages smart contracts to automatically calculate carbon intensity using IoT sensor data and third-party verification oracles, tracking transportation methods, energy sources, manufacturing processes, and storage conditions. CarbonFlow features a novel Carbon Offset Marketplace where verified reduction activities generate tradeable credits that can be directly applied to product carbon passports for immediate neutralization. The platform utilizes zero-knowledge proofs to maintain a dual-layer architecture supporting both permissioned enterprise networks for sensitive data and public transparency layers for consumer verification.

CarbonFlow delivers immediate supply chain carbon neutralization capabilities while providing regulatory bodies with immutable audit trails and consumers with complete carbon journey histories via QR code scanning. The solution includes advanced features such as Carbon Risk Scoring for predictive compliance monitoring, automated ESG reporting generation, and a Carbon Genome system for tracking carbon DNA through complex multi-tier networks. Integration with existing ERP systems ensures seamless adoption while maintaining data integrity and enabling real-time carbon accountability across entire supply ecosystems.
